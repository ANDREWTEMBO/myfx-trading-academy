Tier 1

Foundation Programme

The essential starting point. Designed for complete beginners, this 8-week curriculum takes you from zero to confident — with the vocabulary, skills, and mindset to trade the market correctly.

Most beginner traders fail not because the market is impossible, but because they start without a structure. They pick up random tips, watch inconsistent YouTube videos, and build a patchwork approach that falls apart under pressure.

Tier 1 solves this. It's a complete, linear, 8-week programme — one concept building on the last — so that by the end, you have a solid, tested foundation to trade from.

"The foundation is not just the beginning — it is the whole building. If this is weak, everything else falls."

You will finish Tier 1 knowing how to read a chart, manage your risk, identify key structures, and approach the market with discipline. This is the non-negotiable prerequisite for everything that follows.
