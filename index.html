Our Philosophy

Trading is a language.

Founded by Andrew Tembo, a Mathematics Lecturer at Mulungushi University in Zambia, myFX is built on the belief that trading mastery comes from understanding, not imitation.

"When you understand what the market is saying, every candle becomes a sentence."
